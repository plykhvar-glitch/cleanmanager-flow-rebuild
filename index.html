What this is

We opened Clean Manager the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Clean Manager: installed it, went through the first screens, read your App Store listing and its in-app purchases, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

The onboarding and paywall are yours: your black, your blue pill, your Photos and iCloud tiles with red badges, your three story screens, your trial toggle and timeline. The new screens reuse the parts of your app people already know: the Storage card, the red GB pills, the red checkboxes and the two-line Delete button.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
